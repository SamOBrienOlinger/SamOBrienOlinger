The processFrom the first conversation to a practical handover.

A thoughtful process.
A practical result.

An agreed scope, something tangible to review, and a practical handover.

  1. 01 / Discover

    Start with the need.

    Agree the audience, purpose, content and what a useful result would look like.

  2. 02 / Shape

    Make it tangible.

    Explore the structure and a working prototype before committing to the full experience.

  3. 03 / Build & review

    Refine the details.

    Develop, check keyboard and mobile use, and bring feedback into the next iteration.

  4. 04 / Handover

    Plan what comes next.

    Document the essentials and agree responsibility for updates, support and content review.
