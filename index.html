A New Life in Ireland

An interactive learning resource exploring migration and settlement through twelve fictional characters. Follow a five-stage journey, consider decisions and explanations, then explore the information hub and Fact or Fiction activities to deepen your understanding.

Explore the project

Interactive learning

Both Sides

A choice-based learning game exploring public rights and Garda decisions in Ireland. Follow fictional encounters, consider a response and read the explanation, then switch perspectives to see the same situation from the other side.
